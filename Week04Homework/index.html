Oblivion vs Skyrim

Why Oblivion is better

  • Oblivion has better sidequests
  • Oblivion has a better magic system
  • Oblivion has more classic RPG elements

Why Skyrim is better

  • Skyrim has better combat
  • Skyrim is more realistic
  • Skyrim has better dungeons

Whether you consider Skyrim or Oblivion better, comes down to play preference. Skyrim is a more modern and polished experience, while Oblivion is a bit more of a classic RPG with more interesting guilds and sidequests.
